\section{\textbf{Research Interests}}
\vspace{1mm}
{
My research focuses on AI for education. My work bridges AI and education by translating educational goals and expert knowledge into task-specific computational tasks. I have developed algorithms, benchmark datasets, and systems that support teaching and learning, including automated assessment and feedback, instructional material evaluation, storybook generation, and special education. My career goal is to develop reliable, responsible AI frameworks that promote equitable, high-quality education worldwide.

\vspace{5pt}
My future research will continue pursuing interdisciplinary collaborations to develop AI applications that address specific educational needs. Building on these collaborations, I will develop a theoretical AI for education framework that translates pedagogical goals into computational tasks, along with an agent-based system to automate parts of task formulation and evaluation design.


\vspace{1mm}
\textbf{Keywords:} AI for Education, Human--AI Collaboration, Large Language Models,
Natural Language Processing,
Automated Assessment and Feedback, AI Literacy, Early Literacy, Special Education
}
