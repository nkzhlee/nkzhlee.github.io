\section{\textbf{Research Interests}}
\vspace{1mm}
{

My research focuses on human-centered AI technologies for education that translate educational goals and expert knowledge into computational tasks. My work bridges AI and education by translating educational goals into task-specific computational goals. I have developed algorithms, benchmark datasets, and systems that support teaching and learning, including automated assessment and feedback in special education, instructional material evaluation, storybook generation, and student behavior analysis. My career goal is to develop reliable and responsible AI frameworks to promote equitable, high-quality education worldwide.

\vspace{5pt}
My future research will continue pursuing interdisciplinary collaborations to develop AI applications that address specific educational needs. Building on these collaborations, I will develop a theoretical framework for translating pedagogical goals into computational tasks and an agent-based system to automate parts of task formulation and evaluation design. I will also investigate how to use AI responsibly in education, focusing on potential risks such as emotional overreliance on AI and declines in students' independent reasoning. These directions will inform the design of educational technologies that support learning and human connection in the era of AI.

I have published in leading venues such as \textit{EMNLP}, \textit{AAAI}, \textit{AIED}, \textit{BJET}, and \textit{Computers \& Education}, and have contributed to NSF proposals and served as an NSF panelist.
