Background

From NLP to educational AI

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Penn State in 2024, advised by Rebecca J. Passonneau. My doctoral research focused on automated and semi-automated assessment of STEM reasoning.

At the University at Buffalo, I led AI development for exceptional education and early literacy. I am now a postdoctoral associate at UT San Antonio, working with Dr. Jinjun Xiong. My long-term goal is reliable, responsible AI that promotes equitable, high-quality education.
