About my work

Research grounded in real classrooms and communities.

I design datasets and AI systems that support educators, children, and families. Current work includes automated assessment of parent-child interactions, early reading tools, evaluation of K–12 science materials, and child-centered multimodal learning experiences.

I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Penn State in 2024, advised by Rebecca J. Passonneau. My dissertation on assessing STEM reasoning questions received a Best Doctoral Dissertation Award.
