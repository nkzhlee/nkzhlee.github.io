Selected research experience

  1. Ongoing project · July 2025–present

    01. SciEval: evaluating K–12 science instructional materials

    Role
    Research lead
    Mentor
    Jinjun Xiong (The University of Texas at San Antonio)
    Collaborators
    Tingting Li (Washington State University); Peng He (Washington State University)

  3. Ongoing project · Nov 2024–present

    03. Embodied AI: reading companions for children's AI literacy

    Role
    Research lead
    Mentor
    Jinjun Xiong (The University of Texas at San Antonio)
    Collaborator
    Shiyan Jiang (University of Pennsylvania)

    I implement pedagogically structured dialogue for an LLM-powered reading robot. The feasibility study evaluates dialogue policies with simulated child profiles and an expert; it informs future studies of physical presence, turn-taking, and participation by children and adults. Related Storio studies examine bilingual conversational support in children's e-book reading: a design study with children, parents, and educators, and a separate randomized study comparing AI-guided and parent-led reading across comprehension, vocabulary, retelling, and engagement.

  4. Ongoing project · Nov 2024–present

    04. PaiCoach: AI assessment and coaching for autism home interventions

    Role
    Research lead
    Mentor
    Jinjun Xiong (The University of Texas at San Antonio)
    Center
    National AI Institute for Exceptional Education (AI4EE / AI4ExceptionalEd) (University at Buffalo)

    I lead ASD-HI benchmark and PaiCoach model development to assess parent–child home interventions and generate feedback for specialist review. ASD-HI benchmarks communication-strategy detection and assessment. A four-family PaiCoach study found preliminary improvements under an AI-assisted workflow in which specialists corrected strategy codes and reviewed every feedback message before delivery.

    • Submitted to LAK 2027: A new dataset for benchmarking speech recognition for autistic children.
    • Proposal in preparation: NSF Future CoRe.
    • Funded extension: Co-PI on the $50,000 OAR-funded PAC.AI project for AI-supported parent coaching (February 2026–December 2027).

    Also connects with: Pedagogical alignmentHuman-AI collaboration

  7. Doctoral research · Aug 2019–May 2024

    07. STEM assessment datasets and human scoring rubrics

    Role
    Research lead
    Mentor
    Rebecca J. Passonneau (The Pennsylvania State University)
    Collaborator
    Matthew D. Beckman (The Pennsylvania State University)

    I contributed to the I-STUDIO and physics lab-report datasets, including rubric development and human-scoring procedures for automated assessment. Related research compares routine physics classroom grades with trained raters' judgments, showing why target labels need scrutiny and how detailed rubrics make expectations and evidence explicit before those judgments become targets for machine learning.

  8. Doctoral research · Aug 2019–May 2024

    08. Human–AI collaboration: selective prediction and human deferral

    Role
    Research lead
    Mentor
    Rebecca J. Passonneau (The Pennsylvania State University)
    Collaborator
    Sadhana Puntambekar (University of Wisconsin–Madison)

    I developed methods for automated assessment models to answer or defer to a person, including joint optimization of assessment and deferral representations. The initial work evaluates a learned deferral policy in a held-out simulation using human reference labels. Subsequent work jointly trains assessment and deferral representations across four student-answer datasets.

    Also connects with: Pedagogical alignment

    Related papers & resources

    • Joint Training for Selective Prediction2024 · arXiv:2410.24029
    • Learning When to Defer to Humans for Short Answer Grading2023 · AIED 2023, pp. 414–425

Additional & earlier work

Earlier work · 2018-2019

Machine reading comprehension

This work develops hierarchical methods for answering questions across multiple passages. Passage selection, question-relevant sentence selection, and answer extraction break the task into stages. Reinforcement learning for main-content selection examines how choosing useful evidence can improve downstream reading-comprehension models on benchmark datasets.

Related papers & resources

  • Teaching Machines to Extract Main Content for Machine Reading Comprehension2019 · AAAI 2019, 33(1), 9973–9974
  • Hierarchical Answer Selection Framework for Multi-passage Machine Reading Comprehension2018 · China Conference on Information Retrieval (CCIR), pp. 93–104

Earlier work · 2016

Dataflow tools for machine learning

A visual dataflow system makes machine-learning workflows easier to construct, execute, monitor, and reuse. Users connect operations as a directed acyclic graph in a drag-and-drop studio. The system integrates algorithm libraries with Hadoop and Spark infrastructure to support both standalone and distributed computation.

Related papers & resources

  • Ease the Process of Machine Learning with Dataflow2016 · CIKM 2016, pp. 2437–2440

Earlier work · 2014

Sentiment-aware word representations

This early NLP work incorporates sentiment labels into a semi-supervised language model for learning word vectors. Experiments on English and Chinese product reviews examine how semantic and sentiment information work together, studying representation choices that support review classification across the two languages.

Related papers & resources

  • Word Vector Modeling for Sentiment Analysis of Product Reviews2014 · NLPCC 2014, pp. 168–180
