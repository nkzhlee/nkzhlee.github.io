Selected research

Projects

I work with educators, learning scientists, and AI researchers to turn research ideas into tools that can be studied in real settings.

Exceptional education

AI-supported home intervention assessment

Leading work on a parent-child interaction dataset and multimodal AI pipeline for assessing home-based interventions for autistic children, with parent-facing feedback.

National AI Institute for Exceptional Education · University at Buffalo

Early literacy

AI Reading Enhancer

Developing personalized decodable book generation and evaluation with early literacy researchers, grounded in K–2 reading practices.

Center for Early Literacy and Responsible AI · University at Buffalo

AI literacy

Embodied reading companions

Exploring multimodal models and robotic agents to support young children’s reading and AI literacy, with an emphasis on child-appropriate interactions.

Science education

Evaluating K–12 instructional materials

Building human-calibrated datasets and evidence-based AI evaluation methods for the quality of science lessons and instructional materials.

In collaboration with researchers at Washington State University

Art education

LLM4Art

Studying how large language models can support art interpretation through curated data, evaluation, and an educational prototype.

Inclusive learning

LLM4Simulation

Exploring interactive dyslexia simulations that bring together language models and immersive learning environments for awareness and training.
