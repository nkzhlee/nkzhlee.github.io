Aligning AI with pedagogical goals

Models, rubrics, and benchmarks that align AI judgments with educational goals.

Ongoing project · July 2025-present

Evaluating K-12 science instructional materials

SciEval and Judging the Judges examine how language models evaluate K-12 science lessons using educator-defined criteria. SciEval supplies a rubric-based benchmark with scores and evidence; expert review in Judging the Judges identifies where model scores, explanations, and suggestions depart from instructional context.

Research papers · 2021-2023

Short-answer assessment and formative feedback

SFRN and AsRRN represent relationships among questions, reference answers, and student responses for automated assessment. Related work on statistics responses contributes the I-STUDIO dataset, human-scoring procedures, and exploratory feedback grouping, connecting model design with reliable labels and the instructional purpose of formative feedback.

Research papers · 2022-2023

Reliable assessment of physics lab reports

This research pairs a physics lab-report dataset with a study of rubric-based assessment reliability. Comparing routine classroom grades with trained raters' judgments shows why the target label needs scrutiny. Detailed rubrics make expectations and evidence more explicit before those judgments become targets for machine learning.

Research papers · 2025

LLM assessment of science essays

This study examines how demonstration examples guide language models in identifying rubric-defined scientific ideas in student essays. It tests the number and quality of examples used in prompts, treating example design as part of the assessment task rather than relying only on general model capabilities.

Related papers & resources

Ongoing project · July 2025-present

LLM4Art: AI-assisted art interpretation

LLM4Art connects art scholars' expertise with language-model interpretation through prompts and evaluation rubrics for visual analysis, historical context, and cultural significance. A benchmark and prototype application support comparison of model interpretations, with ongoing work on art-focused datasets, model adaptation, and educational uses.

Research papers · 2023-2024

Selective prediction and human deferral

These studies ask when an automatic grader should predict and when it should defer to a person. The initial work evaluates a learned deferral policy in a held-out simulation using human reference labels. Subsequent work jointly trains assessment and deferral representations across four student-answer datasets.

Research papers · 2025

Storio: AI-guided dialogic reading

Two Storio studies examine bilingual conversational support during children's e-book reading. A design study explores responsive scaffolding with children, parents, and educators; a separate randomized study compares AI-guided and parent-led reading. Together they distinguish comprehension, vocabulary, retelling, and engagement to inform the design of learning partners.

An LLM-powered humanoid robot provides a platform for shared reading and early childhood AI literacy. The feasibility study evaluates structured dialogue policies with simulated child profiles and an expert. Personalized learning and special education

Materials, interactions, and feedback adapted to learners' goals and needs, with teachers, specialists, and families involved.

Ongoing project · Jan 2025-present

StoryLab and AIRE: personalized reading materials

StoryLab combines teacher-defined learning goals with children's interests to generate stories, illustrations, and vocabulary support. AIRE extends this work to personalized decodable texts aligned with taught phonics patterns. Literacy experts' rubrics guide generation and review, while teachers can select and revise materials for their instruction.

Ongoing project · Nov 2024-present

ASD-HI and PaiCoach: support for parent coaching

ASD-HI benchmarks detection and assessment of communication strategies in parent-child home interventions for autistic children. PaiCoach builds on this work to generate feedback tailored to shared-reading sessions. A four-family study found preliminary improvements under an AI-assisted workflow in which specialists corrected strategy codes and reviewed every feedback message before delivery.

Research lead

LLM4Simulation: dyslexia simulations

This ongoing collaboration investigates dyslexia simulation technologies for public awareness and special education training. The work includes a review of existing approaches and development of an LLM-based prototype for interactive activities, with immersive environments combining virtual reality and AI agents as a direction under development.

Earlier work in NLP and machine learning

Foundations in machine reading comprehension, sentiment representations, and tools for building machine-learning workflows.

Earlier work · 2018-2019

Machine reading comprehension

This work develops hierarchical methods for answering questions across multiple passages. Passage selection, question-relevant sentence selection, and answer extraction break the task into stages. Reinforcement learning for main-content selection examines how choosing useful evidence can improve downstream reading-comprehension models on benchmark datasets.

Related papers & resources

Earlier work · 2016

Dataflow tools for machine learning

A visual dataflow system makes machine-learning workflows easier to construct, execute, monitor, and reuse. Users connect operations as a directed acyclic graph in a drag-and-drop studio. The system integrates algorithm libraries with Hadoop and Spark infrastructure to support both standalone and distributed computation.

Related papers & resources

  • Ease the Process of Machine Learning with Dataflow2016 · conference

Earlier work · 2014

Sentiment-aware word representations

This early NLP work incorporates sentiment labels into a semi-supervised language model for learning word vectors. Experiments on English and Chinese product reviews examine how semantic and sentiment information work together, studying representation choices that support review classification across the two languages.

Related papers & resources

Future directions

Building on these projects, I plan to pursue three connected directions:

  • Educational goals to AI tasks: LLM agents that propose rubrics and evaluation specifications for educator review.
  • AI that strengthens human connection: Agents that invite teacher, peer, and family participation while supporting independent thinking.
  • Personalized special-education support: Materials and coaching aligned with educator-approved Individualized Education Program (IEP) goals, with specialist review.
