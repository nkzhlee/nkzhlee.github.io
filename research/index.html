Future directions

My future research will continue pursuing interdisciplinary collaborations to develop AI applications that address specific educational needs. Building on these collaborations, I will develop a theoretical AI for education framework that translates pedagogical goals into computational tasks, along with an agent-based system to automate parts of task formulation and evaluation design.
